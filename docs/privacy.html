Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-21

Klipje Uploader ("the app") is a personal-use automation tool built and operated solely by its developer to publish short-form video content to the developer's own social media accounts (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram). It is not distributed or offered to the public as a service.

What data the app accesses

The app connects, via each platform's official API, only to social media accounts owned and controlled by the developer. It requests the minimum permissions needed to:

  • Upload and schedule video content to the developer's own YouTube channel(s)
  • Upload and publish video content to the developer's own TikTok account(s)
  • Upload and publish video content to the developer's own Instagram account(s)

The app does not access, collect, or process data belonging to any other user or third party.

Data storage and sharing

Authentication tokens are stored locally by the developer and are never shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. No data collected through these connections is used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the developer's own accounts.

Data retention

Tokens and any locally cached video/content metadata are retained only as long as needed to operate the app, and are deleted when a connection is revoked.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the respective platform's account security settings (Google Account, TikTok, or Meta Accounts Center).
